# Accessibility Audit

Check that UI works for keyboard and screen-reader users.

## Checklist

- All interactive elements are reachable and operable by keyboard.
- Images have meaningful `alt` text; decorative images have empty `alt`.
- Color contrast meets WCAG AA.
- Form inputs have associated labels; errors are announced.
- Landmarks and heading order are logical.

Test with a screen reader and keyboard-only navigation, not just automated tools.
